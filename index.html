Networking concepts

Think of the internet link as a road and data as the cars using it.

Bandwidth

The maximum rate at which data could travel, measured in Mbps.

How many lanes the road has.

Throughput

The rate at which data really gets through. It is usually lower than bandwidth.

How many cars actually pass each minute.

Congestion

When demand is more than the link can carry, causing delay, queues and lost packets.

A traffic jam at rush hour.

Capacity

The total bandwidth available on the office link for everyone to share.

The most traffic the road can hold.
